Welcome to JEO Research!

I am a researcher that open-sources software related to eye tracking, augmented reality, virtual reality, and other user interface technology. This site is organized into tutorials around these topics that are designed to improve accessibility and DIY instructions. Each topic has sample code, text tutorials, images, and/or videos for you to get started.
